# PRODUCT.md — RUXs

## Audience
Households looking for nearby daily essentials and family services, especially people coordinating regular meals, water or dairy deliveries and caregivers finding activities for children. Independent neighbourhood providers also use RUXs to manage the supported parts of their business.

## Product purpose
Make neighbourhood services easier to discover and recurring household routines easier to manage. RUXs includes daily essentials such as tiffin meals, water cans, milk and local stores, plus a wider service marketplace including kids' learning, DIY kits, gifts, mehndi, fitness and physiotherapy.

## Main tasks
- Households: find relevant nearby providers, compare each provider's own prices and terms, and continue through available order, subscription or booking flows.
- Families: explore children’s learning, activities, camps and kits by the available provider and age group.
- Merchants: manage the workflows supported for their vendor type, including relevant products/services, customers, orders or bookings, recurring plans and billing.
- Admins: configure categories, providers and operations without disturbing household flows.

## Design constraints
- Mobile-first, starting at a 360px viewport.
- Financial and fulfilment correctness outrank visual embellishment.
- Availability, schedules, pricing and service areas depend on the provider; never imply universal coverage.
- The customer app is an **Operate** surface: scanability, consistency and task speed matter.
- The public homepage is a **Persuade** surface: it can use broader story-led illustrations and more brand expression.
- Kids Zone can be playful, but remain aligned with RUXs cream, terracotta, leaf and forest-green brand identity.

## Voice
Warm, clear, practical, neighbourly. Use short labels and plain language. Avoid exaggerated claims, jargon and pushy conversion copy.

## Visual anti-references
Generic blue-purple gradients, glass effects, heavy shadows under every card, repeated cards inside cards, oversized empty-state emoji, tiny low-contrast metadata, and interface patterns that look like a generic AI-generated SaaS dashboard.
