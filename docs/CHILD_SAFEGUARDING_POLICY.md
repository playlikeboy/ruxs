# RUXs Child Safeguarding & Safety Policy

## 1. Principles & Purpose

Children's safety, dignity, and wellbeing are absolute priorities across the RUXs platform.
Because RUXs connects families with home tutors, activity coaches, dance choreographers, and workshop organizers, we distinguish between **services delivered to children** vs **physical goods delivered to households**.

---

## 2. In-Home Service Verification & Rules

### A. Mandatory Adult Guardian Supervision
- **Guardian Presence Requirement**: For all private in-home sessions (e.g., academic tuition, music/dance tutoring, chess coaching), an adult parent or legal guardian MUST remain on the premises in an adjacent or shared space throughout the entire session.
- **Service Mode Transparency**: Provider profiles explicitly badge sessions that require guardian presence (`🏡 Guardian-Present Session`).

### B. Educator & Mentor Identity Verification
Before any provider offering in-home or physical sessions is activated:
1. **Government ID & Address Verification (KYC)**.
2. **Police Verification Certificate / Background Check**: Providers upload official verification certificates which are reviewed and badged on their public storefront.
3. **Academic & Professional Credentials**: Degrees, diploma certificates, and competition awards are authenticated and showcased with viewer links for parents.

---

## 3. Physical Goods & DIY Kits Safety

Vendors offering physical learning products (`kids_products`):
1. **Non-Toxic Material Guarantee**: All paints, clays, adhesives, slime ingredients, and chemicals in science kits must be certified 100% non-toxic.
2. **Age-Appropriate Warnings**: Clear minimum age badges (e.g. `Ages 4–8`) to prevent choking hazards from small parts.
3. **Blunt & Safe Tools**: Scissors, carving tools, and electronics components included in kits must be child-safe or clearly designated for adult-assisted use.

---

## 4. Zero-Tolerance & Immediate Delisting

RUXs enforces immediate suspension upon any report of:
- Inappropriate communication, unapproved contact outside platform channels, or private solicitations.
- Failure to adhere to guardian presence standards during home visits.
- Misrepresentation of certifications or background documentation.

Parents can raise emergency safety flags directly via the RUXs Help & Support Center (`/app/support`).
